About

About Me

I received my B.S. degree in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from Qiuzhen College, Tsinghua University. I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. at Tsinghua University through a joint doctoral training arrangement under the supervision of Prof. Gao Huang.

My research interests primarily lie in LLM post-training, agentic AI, and reasoning.

Research

Research Interests

LLM Post-Training

Reinforcement learning, on-policy distillation, reward design, and data-efficient adaptation for large language models.

Agentic AI

Learning, self-critique, tool use, and autonomous problem solving for language-model agents.

Reasoning

Improving mathematical and scientific reasoning through post-training, reinforcement learning, and distillation.

NeurIPS 2026

From Reasoning Chains to Verifiable Subproblems: Curriculum Reinforcement Learning Enables Credit Assignment for LLM Reasoning

Xitai Jiang, Zihan Tang, Wenze Lin, Yang Yue, Shenzhi Wang, Gao Huang
