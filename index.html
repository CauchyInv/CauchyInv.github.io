About

About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at Tsinghua University, advised by [Advisor Name]. My research lies at the intersection of large language models, reinforcement learning, and reasoning.

I am particularly interested in how curriculum design, intermediate supervision, and fine-grained credit assignment can improve the reasoning capabilities of language models. I am also interested in understanding generation behavior and failure modes in modern generative models.

Previously, I received my B.S. degree from [University], where I worked on the training dynamics and generalization of neural networks.

Research

Research Interests

A broad overview of the research directions I am currently exploring.

Large Language Models

Learning, reasoning, post-training, alignment, and evaluation of foundation models.

Reinforcement Learning

RL for reasoning, verifiable rewards, policy optimization, and efficient post-training.

Mathematical Reasoning

Long-horizon reasoning, verifiable problem solving, and curriculum-based training.

Credit Assignment

Fine-grained, token-level, and process-aware learning signals for complex trajectories.

Generative Models

Understanding local generation bias, hallucination, and failure modes in diffusion models.

Learning Dynamics

Optimization, generalization, symmetry, and nonlinear dynamics of neural networks.
